How it works

Three taps and you are browsing.

Install it

One file, nothing else to fetch. It opens on the home screen with your own shortcuts.

02

Make it your browser

03

Browse

Ads are blocked before they load. Point the camera at any text to copy it or search it.

The camera

Point. Press once. Take the words.

A page, a form, a sign, a receipt. Press the shutter and the picture holds still while the text is read off it. Long press a word, drag the handles, and the whole line is yours to copy or search.

What is inside

Everything here is in the build you download.

EyeBlock

A real content blocker inside the app, not a list of addresses. Ads and trackers are cancelled before they leave the page.

Blocked count

Open the menu and see how many requests were stopped on the page you are reading.

Per-site switch

A site that breaks gets one tap to stand down. Every other site stays protected.

Camera scanner

Reads the text in front of you, holds the picture still, and lets you pick the words.

First run, done right

Sets itself as your browser and shows where the old one can be turned off.

Shortcuts that stay put

Your own sites, pinned on the home screen, in the order you put them.

Privacy

Nothing about you leaves the phone.

Eyebrowse does not ask for an account and does not send your pages anywhere. Blocking runs on the device, the scanner reads the picture on the device, and the words you select stay where you put them.
