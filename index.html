What it does, without the adjectives.

Every line below is in the build you download. Nothing is promised that is not already there.

01

EyeBlock stops ads at the source

A full content blocker inside the app, not a list of blocked addresses. Ad and tracker requests are cancelled before they leave the page.

02

The camera reads the page in front of you

Press the shutter, the picture holds still, the words come off it. Long press a word, drag the handles, and copy or search the whole line.

03

You can see what was stopped

The menu shows how many requests were blocked on the page you are reading.

04

One site can stand down

A page that breaks gets a single tap to turn protection off for that site only.

05

First run does the setting up

It asks to be your browser and shows where the old one can be turned off.

06

Home stays yours

The sites you pin stay where you put them. Nothing is shipped by default that you did not ask for.
